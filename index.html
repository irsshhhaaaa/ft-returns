Can I start with just one service?

Yes. Start with VAT, accounting or business finance, and discuss additional support as your needs change.

What records should I prepare?

Start with invoices, bank statements and any existing accounting records. We will explain what else is needed for the agreed work.

How is pricing agreed?

The fee depends on your business, transaction volume and the support you need. We agree a clear scope and fee after the consultation.

Can you help with VAT registration?

Registration support is part of our VAT services. Contact us to discuss your business and the information needed to assess your position.

Who is ft. Returns designed for?

Owner-operated businesses and growing SMEs in the UAE, including trading, service, retail and hospitality businesses.
